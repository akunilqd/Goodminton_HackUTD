How analysis works

Three steps, described plainly. Where the system is unsure, the report says so, and estimated shots are drawn differently from tracked ones.

  1. 1

    Upload a match recording

    A single fixed camera behind a baseline works best, with all four court corners in view.

  2. 2

    Play is tracked and organised into rallies

    The court lines set the scale. Players and shuttle are tracked, and play is split into rallies with a rebuilt score.

  3. 3

    Review, inspect, choose what to rewatch

    Find the runs that swung a game, open any rally at its timestamp, and save the moments worth a second look.

What to expect

These limits are provisional. They will be confirmed once the processing service has been measured on real recordings.

Video formats
MP4 or MOV, up to 4 GB and 120 minutes
Processing time
Not yet measured. You’ll see each step as it runs, and you can leave the page.
Recording
One fixed, raised camera; whole court in frame; 720p and 30 fps or better
Match type
Singles only in this version
Known gaps
Fast exchanges can lose the shuttle; those shots are marked “estimated”. Shot types are labelled from flight and landing, so check them against the footage.
